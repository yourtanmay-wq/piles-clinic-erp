# NEW STAFF — JOINING INFORMATION CHECKLIST
### Maa Ayurved Piles Clinic / TK Biswas Piles Clinic
**Branch:** Kishanganj  ·  **Prepared:** 27 August 2026  ·  **Version:** V734

> Give this sheet to the new staff member (or to the Sister/in-charge collecting
> the details). Every row below is an actual field in our app — nothing extra is
> asked, and nothing that the app needs is left out.

---

## A. FOR LOGIN — required before the person can use the app

| # | What we need | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Mobile number (10 digits)** | This IS the login ID. Must be the person's own SIM, in daily use. |
| A2 | **Confirm the number is on WhatsApp** | Patient/doctor messages open through WhatsApp. |
| A3 | **Full name in English (CAPITAL letters)** | Exactly as it should appear on records. |
| A4 | **Passport-size photo** | Clear face, plain background. Phone photo is fine. |

*(The staff code name — e.g. **KNE-KISHAN7** — is decided by the clinic, not by the staff member.)*

## F. LANGUAGE — one question that matters for this branch

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Can the person read Bengali?** | The app has a setting that removes all Bengali from the screen for a staff member who cannot read it. It was made for the previous Kishanganj staff. If the new person also cannot read Bengali, tell us — we will switch it on for their number. If they can read Bengali, nothing needs to change. |

### Please also confirm the leaving of the previous staff member

In the app: **Master → Staff Profiles → (that profile) → Remove**.
That one button stops the login, removes the name from the Salary Due list, and
turns off the income/expense key. **No past record is deleted.**
